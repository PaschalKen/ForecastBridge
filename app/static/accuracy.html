"What happened" is Open-Meteo's historical weather (ERA5: weather-model data corrected with observations), which covers areas about 30 km across, while each forecast is for the city's exact point. So part of the error is a steady difference between the two rather than a wrong forecast: that's the bias (+ means forecasts ran warmer). An hour counts as rainy with 0.1 mm of rain or more.
